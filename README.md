# Elturel in Avernus

Open [index.html](index.html) in a WebGL-capable browser. The explorer runs offline, including its textures and sounds. Keep the files together when moving the app.

## Explore

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD, Shift, Space, C | Walk, run, jump and crouch |
| F | Toggle flight. W and S move forward and backward. Shift accelerates, Space rises and C descends. |
| WASD, Shift, Space, C in liquid | Swim in any horizontal direction, swim faster, rise, or dive |
| V | Release or capture the cursor. Drag the landscape to look around with the cursor free. |
| E, T | Use a nearby interactive door or toggle your portable light |
| M, K | Open the area map or toggle the minimap |
| 1, 2, 3 | First person, isometric and top-down |
| Esc | Journey menu with save, load and return to title |

The first-person elevation readout includes your compass bearing. The player marker shows your viewing angle on the minimap and area map. North points down on the High Hall floor plans. Walking has quiet contact sounds on stone, loose ground and timber docks. Swimming in water has a separate stroke sound. Movement sounds follow the ambient volume and mute controls.

On the full area map, **GM reference numbers** toggles the explorer’s city marker numbers and the published room keys on labeled interior plans. This setting starts off and is saved with your journey. Place names have their own control in **World**.

Select a landmark or open ground on the area map, then choose **Quick travel**. **World** controls lighting, weather, vision and range, ambient volume, and place names. The title screen resumes the last local journey. A portable JSON save preserves position, camera and survey framing, lighting, weather, and vision.

Choose **Above the Styx** for a flying start outside the city, **Eastern Chain Anchor** for the view from the plain, or **High Hall Vista** for the view from the eastern timber landing. Elevation is measured in feet relative to the river. The area map has optional 25-foot elevation contours. Occasional imps cross the sky during outdoor exploration.

Quick travel to **High Hall** and follow the flagstones around the spring to the cathedral steps. Walk through the pointed doorway or press **E** at the sill. The interior loads on entry. Cross the threshold on your way out to return to the courtyard. Journeys saved inside reopen on the same level.

The twin winding stairs in the foyer lead to the choir gallery, pipe organ, defenses, and balcony. The eastern stair beyond the sanctuary leads down to the catacombs. Follow the galleries to the Hall of Scholars, the Unknown Hero’s tomb, the main crypts, and the escape tunnel. Survey views reveal the floor you occupy. From anywhere inside, **Top-down → VTT export** lets you select **Cathedral**, **Choir**, or **Catacombs** for a full-floor PNG and its scale data. **Current area** exports the encounter you framed.

At the choir balcony, press **E** beside the double doors, then walk through to the parapet. The doors work from either side and their position is saved with your journey. The balcony is part of the city. Press **F** outside, rise above the parapet with **Space**, and fly over the rooftops. Return to the balcony above the cathedral entrance, descend with **C**, toggle **F** to land, and walk back through the choir doors. Follow H17 to the end of the escape tunnel and walk through the rock opening. The path continues onto a stone ledge overlooking the River Styx. Turn back through the same opening to return to the catacombs.

At the choir organ console, press **E** to play. Use the displayed A–K keyboard letters or press the on-screen keys with a mouse or touch. Close the organ panel or press **Esc** to stop playing. Organ sound follows the volume and ambient-audio setting in **World**.

Press **E** at the lever beside Torm’s teak altar to open the fist and reveal the hidden stair. Enter from the front of the dais. The passage turns at a second release handle before joining the central crypt. Either handle operates the altar, and your journey file records its position.

Inside High Hall, **M** opens the floor you occupy. Choose another floor to inspect it, or select open floor and choose **Travel within High Hall**. Zoom out past the floor plan to reach the Elturel map. **Show High Hall map** returns to the interior plan.

Quick travel to **Grand Cemetery** and follow the central path through the broken brass gate. Walk through the chapel doors to enter. The vestment chamber stairs lead to the workshop and the ossuaries. **M** opens the cemetery floor plan. **Top-down → VTT export** offers full chapel and ossuary maps inside, and **Grand Cemetery grounds** in the city.

Four family mausoleums stand in the cemetery grounds. At a mausoleum's north door press **E**, then walk inside. At the shelf on the far wall press **E** to read the family memorial. The doors work from both sides and their positions are saved. In city **Top-down → VTT export**, select a family mausoleum to export a roofless encounter map and its scale data.

The four family histories are original to this explorer. Their founders were rolled with Zach Best’s *Universal NPC Emulator*. Their names came from [Fantasy Name Generators’ English Renaissance generator](https://www.fantasynamegenerators.com/english-renaissance-names.php).

Open the area map with **M** to visit **Mugge’s Net Loft** in the Dock District, **Stace’s Laundry** in Westerly, or **Holcot House** on Orrel Stair. Use **E** at the street door, walk inside, and approach the worktable to inspect an object. Rolf Mugge, Petronelle Stace and Aldreda Holcot are original residents named with the same generator. Their trades draw on the canal freight traffic, West Docks inns and western ascent in the Alexandrian gazetteer.

**Fire rain** adds wind-driven burning drops and a quiet crackle. Companion storms have steady arcs and distant thunder. Reduced-motion mode hides the arcs, falling particles and imp flights. Blindsight can accompany any sight choice and has its own range. Darkvision keeps normal lighting in color and reveals dark surfaces in gray.

## A Pair of Black Antlers

**A Pair of Black Antlers** is on the city area map in the Dock District. Quick travel takes you to its street sign. Press **E** at the door to enter. At a chair or stool, press **Q** to sit. **E**, **Q**, or a movement key stands you up. **E** reads the bill at the bar and operates the guest-room doors.

Take a tankard with **E**, walk around the counter into the bartender’s aisle, face a tap and press **E** to pour. Carry the ale to your stool and use **G** to set it on the counter. **Q** lets you sit or stand while holding it.

Aim at a movable chair, stool, table, crate or barrel and press **G**. Use the wheel to adjust reach, **Shift + wheel** to lift or lower it, and **R** to rotate. Click or press **G** to place it, or press **Escape** to cancel. In a survey view, use the pointer to choose a spot.

The southeast stair leads to the guest floor. A second stair leads up to the loft. The northwest kitchen landing turns onto the cellar stairs, where the shelf on the north wall below conceals a passage. The taproom and guest floor each have a privy. Each of the four floors has an area map and a VTT export. Journey files record the furniture, tankards and their contents, room doors, cellar shelf and your seat.

The interior follows the supplied floor plan, fitted to the existing building. Ontharr Frume’s connection to the Order of the Gauntlet informs the meeting room. ## Export an encounter map

1. Switch to **Top-down**. Drag to pan and scroll to zoom. Double-click a roof to frame a building. Use **Align building** with the building near the center to square it with the grid.
2. Open **VTT export** and choose **Current area**. Enter the width and height in feet and choose the pixel density. Select a baked grid or leave the PNG gridless for Foundry or Roll20.
3. Choose **Download PNG**. **Download scale data** records the physical and image dimensions, pixels per 5 feet along both axes, rotation, and grid origin. Set the VTT map to the exported dimensions and use that scale. The image exporter caps the longest edge at 4,096 pixels.
4. For a furnished ground floor, select the house by name and choose its **interior** preset. The exporter lifts the roof for the map. Street and survey views keep the roofs in place.

The ruler measures horizontal distance, bearing and elevation change. The default survey grid is 5-foot squares. **Scale with zoom** shifts to hexes at city scale. Hex size is measured flat to flat.

## References and interpretation

The cemetery chapel and ossuary follow Maps 2.5 and 2.6. The curved vestry stair reaches the lower galleries and the Vault of Ascendance. Both floors and the exterior grounds have separate VTT export presets. The chapel windows, cemetery mosaics and frescoes, and High Hall lancets use original image textures bundled for offline play.

The layout follows the fallen city in Chapter 2 and the geography of the Alexandrian Remix. The model uses an interpreted street plan at a consistent physical scale. Landmarks include the High Hall, Grand Cemetery, Torm's bridges, the eastern canal docks, and the western switchback. Vesh House, Bell's Wash Yard and Orrel Stair are original places. Most buildings are exterior shells sized for future interiors. Sol 6.2 and Terra 6.1 created six original surface material sets. The house masonry, plaster, timber grain and pitched roof maps also draw on this workspace's Greenest material library.
